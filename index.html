    Control, not a verdict

    Envictica does not determine the substantive correctness of the work. It determines whether the operational conditions required for use are present.

    Control conditions

    • Authoritative source attached and verified
    • AI-to-AI validation or dependency chains identified
    • Accountable human owner identified
    • Substantive human review completed before use

    Decision states

    ALLOW — operating conditions met.

    REVIEW — human attention is required.

    INTERCEPT — do not deploy or rely on the output.
