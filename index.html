    Control, not a verdict

    Envictica does not decide the substance of the work. It determines whether the operational conditions exist to safely use the output.

    What is checked

    • Authoritative source attachment and verification
    • AI-to-AI validation or dependency chains
    • A named accountable human owner
    • Completed human review before use

    Decision states

    ALLOW — operating conditions met.

    REVIEW — human attention is required.

    INTERCEPT — do not deploy or rely on this output.
